Purpose and related policies

This Document Handling Policy forms part of the Terms of Use and explains the handling of customer COIs, requirements, extracted information, and reports. See our Privacy Policy for account information, providers, and individual requests.

Submission and authority

Submit only COIs, endorsements, requirements, vendor lists, renewal records, and information needed for your review. Confirm that you may share them and remove unrelated sensitive information. Use the intake method agreed during onboarding; do not publish documents to public repositories, shared public folders, or public AI tools.

Review and confidentiality

The self-hosted model runs in a U.S. AWS region. Review work may also take place on private work computers. The owner or an employee manually reviews every report before delivery; review labor is United States-based. Customer data is confidential, used only for the contracted service and related support, security, and legal obligations, and not used for model training or fine-tuning. Access is limited by job need. We do not submit documents to public LLMs or intentionally make them publicly accessible.

Providers and authorized recipients

The existing web intake uses Uploadcare, with account and upload records in Supabase. AWS supports the review environment; Cloudflare, Resend, and Stripe support other service functions described in the Privacy Policy. Private review does not mean provider-free processing. We share relevant information with the customer’s authorized recipients and with vendors or brokers as necessary for authorized follow-up. Additional processing or retention restrictions require written agreement before intake.

Monthly retention and replacement

Our default retention period for customer documents, extracted document data, working review files, and delivered reports is one calendar month from receipt or creation: the same day of the following month, or that month’s last day if the same day does not exist. At the monthly refresh, the updated set replaces the prior set; we delete the prior versions rather than maintain a historical archive. Copying an old file does not restart its retention period. If no update arrives, the existing set still reaches its deletion date. Before that date, you may request a different schedule; it applies only when we agree in writing to the records covered, duration, and any price change. Please keep your own historical copies. This rule covers inputs, reports, extracted data, and document-bearing working copies. The monthly refresh is not permission to retain superseded versions or indefinitely reset the age of unchanged records. If continued monitoring requires information about a policy that remains in force, the customer must provide or authorize the updated current set before the previous set expires. We explain any resulting monitoring interruption.

Deletion, backups, and cancellation

The retention schedule continues after cancellation and does not reset when service ends. On a valid earlier deletion request, we explain any effect on monitoring and perform deletion subject to applicable law. Deletion must cover the active upload provider, review environment, local working copies, and document-bearing extracts or messages. Backup expiry is a separate, finite schedule disclosed in the service order before intake; we do not promise instantaneous erasure of every backup. Restoration must not return expired records to ongoing use. Narrow legal holds and necessary billing or agreement records are exceptions, not a general archive. Keep your own originals and reports; we are not a long-term records repository.

Different retention arrangements

Request a different schedule at hello@coicomply.com before the current deletion date. We must confirm in writing the covered records, retention period, deletion trigger, backup treatment, and any cost. A request alone does not change the default schedule. After deletion, an extension cannot recover the deleted records.

Corrections and report use

Tell us promptly about an error or new document. We review the issue and provide an appropriate correction; do not silently alter or remove report qualifications. Findings reflect supplied documents and requirements on the review date and do not guarantee coverage, payment of a claim, or vendor eligibility to work. Final vendor and insurance decisions remain with the customer and its qualified advisers.

Report suspected unauthorized access immediately, identifying the affected account and issue without attaching sensitive records to the initial message. We investigate and provide notifications required by applicable law.
