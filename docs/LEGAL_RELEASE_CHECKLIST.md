# COIComply Legal and Commercial Release Checklist

Status: decisions required before public legal pages are finalized.

## Established commercial terms

- Founding membership: $499 per month.
- Availability: first 10 property-management customers.
- Included scope: up to 50 active vendors.
- Initial engagement: 90 days, then month-to-month.
- Founding price remains locked while continuously active and within the original scope.
- Guarantee: notify COIComply within seven days after the first completed compliance report to receive a refund of the first month.
- Service is managed COI administration and reporting, not legal advice, insurance brokerage, underwriting, or a coverage determination.
- Reports are human-reviewed before delivery.
- Customer documents are not used to train general-purpose models.

## Owner decisions required

1. Exact legal entity name and entity type.
2. Principal business mailing address.
3. State of organization.
4. Governing law and exclusive venue.
5. Billing method and invoice due date.
6. Cancellation method and any billing cutoff.
7. File-size limit and accepted types.
8. Privacy/security contact if different from hello@coicomply.com.
9. Whether customers may negotiate a DPA or alternate retention schedule.
10. Whether contractors outside the United States may access documents.

## Terms sections required

- Contracting entity and authority.
- Service description and order-of-precedence rules.
- Founding scope and active-vendor definition.
- Initial term and month-to-month renewal.
- Fees, taxes, failed payments, and scope changes.
- Founding-price lock conditions.
- First-report satisfaction refund.
- Customer ownership and narrow service license.
- Confidentiality and no general-purpose model training.
- Permissions and prohibited uploads.
- Security responsibilities and incident cooperation.
- Subprocessors.
- Suspension, termination, export, and deletion.
- Warranty disclaimers.
- Liability limitations and appropriate indemnification.
- Governing law, venue, notices, assignment, force majeure, severability, waiver, and entire agreement.
- Prospective notice for material changes.

## Privacy sections required

- Operator identity and contact.
- Information categories and sources.
- Processing purposes.
- Authentication, browser storage, IP/device, and security logs.
- Uploaded-document treatment.
- Provider categories and subprocessor link.
- Retention schedule.
- Access, correction, deletion, and request verification.
- Security and incident handling.
- Interstate or international processing as applicable.
- State-specific rights where applicable.
- Effective date and material-change notice.

## Customer-facing document handling sections required

- Accepted and prohibited documents.
- Authentication and access model.
- Private signed delivery.
- Human and automated review.
- General-purpose model-training prohibition.
- Retention and deletion summary.
- Subprocessor link.

- Honest security limitations.

## Counsel review

Counsel should review:

- potential insurance-producer, adjuster, consultant, or other licensing issues;
- liability cap and indemnity;
- governing law, venue, arbitration, and class-action language;
- guarantee and automatic-renewal terms;
- state privacy and breach-notification requirements;
- whether a DPA is advisable or required.
