Subprocessors

Draft for review. Not yet effective.

COIComply uses specialized providers to operate the service. Customer documents are not attached to routine transactional emails or placed in source control.

Cloudflare

Purpose: Website hosting, DNS, traffic security, and server-side authorization functions.
Information: Website requests, IP/device data, authentication headers handled transiently by protected functions, and operational logs configured for the service.

Supabase

Purpose: Authentication, customer-account records, document metadata, and server functions.
Information: Account details, authentication events, document identifiers and metadata, and transactional function data. The current COIComply project is configured in the United States.

Uploadcare

Purpose: Signed document upload, storage, private delivery, and configured safety analysis.
Information: Uploaded customer documents and file metadata.

Resend

Purpose: Transactional email delivery.
Information: Recipient email address, delivery metadata, and non-sensitive confirmation content. Customer document attachments and permanent document URLs are not included.

Stripe

Purpose: Membership checkout, recurring billing, payment processing, refunds, and fraud prevention.
Information: Customer and billing contact details, payment-card information provided directly to Stripe, transaction records, subscription status, and fraud-prevention signals. COIComply does not provide customer documents to Stripe.

GitHub

Purpose: Source control and deployment integration.
Information: Website and application source code. Customer documents and production secrets must not be committed to GitHub.

Changes

COIComply may replace or add providers as the service evolves. Material changes will be reflected here and communicated when required by contract or law.
