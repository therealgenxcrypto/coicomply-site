Retention and deletion

Our default retention period for customer documents, extracted document data, working review files, and delivered reports is one calendar month from receipt or creation: the same day of the following month, or that month’s last day if the same day does not exist. At the monthly refresh, the updated set replaces the prior set; we delete the prior versions rather than maintain a historical archive. Copying an old file does not restart its retention period. If no update arrives, the existing set still reaches its deletion date. Before that date, you may request a different schedule; it applies only when we agree in writing to the records covered, duration, and any price change. Please keep your own historical copies. The monthly rule also applies to document contents in temporary extraction files and review notes. Limited account, billing, contract, consent, and security records may be retained separately as needed to administer the relationship, meet tax or legal requirements, or resolve a specific dispute. We do not use those exceptions to keep a general document archive. A legal hold may preserve relevant records for as long as required. Deletion from active systems is distinct from expiry of residual backup copies; the applicable backup expiry must be established and disclosed in your service order before document intake. Residual copies are restricted to recovery or legal needs and are subject to deletion controls if restored. Contact us before uploading if you require a specific deletion deadline across all copies.

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings; doing so may sign you out or remove the receipt and does not delete server-held records. Infrastructure providers may use necessary security technologies.
