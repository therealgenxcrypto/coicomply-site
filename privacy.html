Who We Are

COIComply is a managed certificate-of-insurance review and monitoring service operated by Turnum LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company. Turnum LLC’s notice address is 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE Ste N, Albuquerque, NM 87110, United States. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to hello@coicomply.com.

Scope

This Policy describes how we handle information through coicomply.com, customer accounts, document intake, reports, support, and related services. It does not govern information a customer handles independently of COIComply.

Information We Collect

Account and business information. We may collect a name, business email, company name, job information, authentication records, account identifiers, customer instructions, and communications.

Customer documents. We receive COIs, endorsements, insurance requirements, vendor lists, renewal documents, spreadsheets, and related notes. These documents may contain vendor contact information, business addresses, insurance policy information, signatures, or other personal information.

Service and device information. We and our providers may process IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, authentication events, pages or functions used, file metadata, error records, and security logs.

Commercial records. We may maintain order forms, invoices, payment status, customer scope, active-vendor counts, renewal dates, and service history. Stripe processes payment-card information. COIComply receives billing and transaction information needed to administer the membership but does not store complete payment-card numbers.

Sources

We receive information from customers and their authorized users, uploaded documents, communications, service providers, authentication systems, and the normal operation of our website and service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and administer the service; authenticate users; receive and organize documents; review apparent COI and endorsement requirements; prepare reports; monitor renewals and exceptions; follow up with vendors when included in the customer scope; provide support; bill and administer customer relationships; prevent fraud and misuse; protect the service; investigate incidents; comply with law; and enforce agreements.

Customer Documents and AI

Customer documents are used to perform the contracted COI review and monitoring service, provide support, maintain security, and satisfy legal obligations. Software and automated extraction may assist the work, but reports are human-reviewed before delivery. COIComply does not use overseas personnel or contractors to review customer documents.

We do not sell customer documents, use them for advertising profiles, or use their contents to train general-purpose artificial-intelligence models. We may use de-identified operational information that does not reveal a customer, vendor, policy, or document to understand service performance.

Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for having authority to provide documents and vendor information. Do not upload Social Security numbers, financial-account information, medical information, driver-license data, unrelated employee records, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for COI review.

Service Providers

We use providers for website hosting and security, authentication and database services, document ingestion and storage, payment processing, transactional email, and related infrastructure. Our current architecture uses Cloudflare, Supabase, Uploadcare, Stripe, Resend, and GitHub. A provider may process information only for contracted services and related legal or security obligations. We may update providers as the service changes and will maintain a current subprocessor list.

Disclosure of Information

We may disclose information to service providers, professional advisers, insurers, auditors, regulators, or law enforcement when reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, to protect rights and security, or to comply with law. We may transfer information in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

Retention

We retain customer documents while needed for active monitoring, disable access promptly after termination or a valid deletion request, and delete them from primary document storage within 30 days unless a legal hold or different written customer agreement applies. Residual encrypted backups may remain until normal provider backup cycles expire, targeted within 90 days.

We may retain limited security logs for up to 12 months, support communications for up to 24 months, and billing, tax, contract, and payment records for seven years or as required by law. We delete, de-identify, or restrict information when the applicable period ends.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed for the nature of the service. The target production architecture requires authenticated uploads, user-specific database authorization, private document delivery through short-lived links, restricted administrative access, and incident-response procedures. No system can guarantee absolute security, and customers should maintain their own authoritative copies.

Your Requests

Authorized users may request access, correction, export, deletion, or account closure by emailing hello@coicomply.com. We may verify identity and organizational authority before acting. We may deny or limit a request when required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute preservation, or another lawful reason, and will explain the basis when appropriate.

State Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and where an individual resides, additional rights may apply. COIComply will respond to verified requests as required by applicable law. Because COIComply is a business-to-business service, some information or requests may be outside the scope of particular consumer privacy laws.

International Processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our providers operate. When legally required, we will use appropriate contractual or other safeguards for cross-border processing.

Children

COIComply is intended for business users and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this Policy as the service, providers, or law changes. We will post the effective date and provide appropriate notice of material changes.

Contact

Privacy and document-handling questions may be sent to hello@coicomply.com or to Turnum LLC, 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE Ste N, Albuquerque, NM 87110, United States.
