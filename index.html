FAQ

Questions property teams usually ask.

Is this software?

COIComply is a managed compliance service. We use software internally, but customers receive hands-on vendor-file cleanup, monitoring, follow-up, and clear reports without a heavy platform rollout.

Who is the founding membership for?

It is designed for property management teams with up to 50 active vendors that want COI administration moved off their internal staff.

What is included in the $499 monthly price?

The founding membership includes initial vendor-file cleanup, COI and endorsement review, expiration and renewal monitoring, vendor follow-up, secure uploads, and recurring compliance reporting.

What requirements do you check against?

We check against the insurance requirements you provide. These may come from leases, vendor contracts, owner standards, property rules, or internal compliance guidelines.

What if we do not have written requirements?

We can still flag obvious issues like expired policies, missing coverage types, incomplete certificates, and upcoming renewals. Your team should confirm final compliance standards with ownership, legal counsel, broker, or risk manager.

Do you review endorsements?

Yes, if endorsements are provided. If a COI references Additional Insured status, Waiver of Subrogation, or Primary and Non-Contributory wording but the endorsement is missing, the report will mark it as not confirmed.

How quickly can we get started?

Onboarding begins after the founding membership is confirmed. Timing for the initial report depends on the size and condition of the vendor file and whether all requirements and documents are available.

How does the satisfaction guarantee work?

If you are not satisfied with your first completed compliance report, tell us within seven days and we will refund your first month.

What counts as an active vendor?

An active vendor is one your team may schedule, approve, pay, or keep on file for work at a managed property. Vendors that no longer work with your properties do not need to remain in the monitored count.

Do you contact vendors for us?

Yes. Vendor follow-up is included in the founding membership. We identify what is missing and work to obtain the required renewal, certificate, or endorsement.

Is pricing month-to-month?

The founding membership begins with a 90-day initial engagement and becomes month-to-month afterward. The $499 founding price remains locked while the account stays active and within its original scope.

What happens if we exceed 50 active vendors?

We will discuss the expanded scope with you before changing anything. You can archive inactive vendors or move to a custom arrangement based on portfolio size and service requirements.

Is this a legal opinion or insurance coverage determination?

No. The report identifies apparent compliance gaps based on the documents and requirements supplied. It is not a legal opinion, coverage determination, or substitute for review by counsel, broker, carrier, or risk manager.
